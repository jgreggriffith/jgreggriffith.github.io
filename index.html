About

Greg Griffith is co-founder and Chief Product Officer of EdgeTheory, a narrative intelligence company that helps government and commercial organizations understand how narratives form, how they spread, and how they shape the decisions that matter most.

At EdgeTheory, he owns the "what": Which capabilities become products, how they look, and how people experience them. His work rests on a conviction: The real value in narrative intelligence lies less in software than in the judgments built into the data, meaning which signals matter, how they're measured, and what they mean for the people who have to act on them.

He writes about narrative intelligence, cognitive warfare, and human nature, and about what the past and present can tell us about the future.
